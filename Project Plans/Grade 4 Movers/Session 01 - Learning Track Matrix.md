# Grade 4 Movers — Unit 1, Session 1 Learning Track Matrix

## Session identity

**Title:** Enter the Welcome Fair  
**Place in unit:** Unit 1, Session 1 of 9  
**Target duration:** 45–50 minutes core learning; optional 10-minute self-study return  
**Primary scene:** `assets/grade-4-movers/big-picture-scenes/01-meet-me.png`  
**Guide character:** the blue learning-companion mascot  
**Narrative purpose:** The mascot wants to meet new friends but has forgotten what belongs on a friend badge. The learner helps it observe the fair, meet several children, and practise a first introduction.

## Session learning objectives

By the end of the session, learners can:

1. identify at least six interests or activities in the Welcome Fair scene;
2. describe a visible person or action using a short intelligible sentence;
3. give their name, age, and one interest in a 15–20 second baseline recording;
4. choose a fair station and give a simple personal reason;
5. use one supported interaction phrase such as “Let me think” or “Could you say that again?”

## Success evidence

- **Comprehension:** 4 of 5 listen-and-find clues located correctly.
- **Productive language:** at least three relevant picture-description sentences.
- **Personal application:** station choice plus a relevant reason.
- **Baseline:** saved introduction containing name/avatar name, age, and one interest.
- **Learner reflection:** chooses an honest confidence response and one next-step goal.

## Recommended track composition

The session should feel like one conversation even though it uses several activity engines.

| Track section | Beats | Approx. time | Packaging recommendation |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Meet the guide | 0–3 | 6 min | One story screen containing several guided-dialogue phases |
| Explore together | 4–5 | 8 min | Dialogue phase flows directly into Explore Hotspots |
| Notice useful language | 6–7 | 9 min | Language in Focus followed by guided oral responses |
| Help solve the mission | 8–9 | 10 min | Listening task plus brief retrieval activity |
| Make it personal | 10–11 | 9 min | Scene-linked speaking choice and baseline recording |
| Reflect and leave | 12–13 | 3 min | One dialogue/reflection screen and narrative bridge |

### Existing learning-track beat mapping

| Intended experience | Current or nearest beat kind |
|---|---|
| Cinematic character conversation | `presentation` or story screen today; proposed `guided_dialogue` |
| Scene exploration | `explore_hotspots` |
| Model sentence noticing | `language_in_focus` |
| Listen for the correct person | `listen_and_choose` initially; `listening_item_match` for fuller Movers form |
| Interest retrieval | `flashcards`, `line_match`, or `memory` |
| Baseline voice response | speaking recorder outside the current composition beat list |
| Reflection and goal | planned reflection screen/card |

The track compiler should eventually accept unsupported planned beats as **placeholders** with objective, instructions, estimated duration, evidence type, and replacement status. This lets curriculum planning precede hard-coded activity formats without losing the intended learning sequence.

## Dialogue script outline

This is a beat outline rather than final voice copy.

| Turn | Character staging | Background focus | Dialogue intent | Student response |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Mascot enters from lower right, approximately 125% stage height, facing left | Full scene dimmed 45% | Warm greeting and surprise at the busy fair | Continue / greeting |
| D2 | Mascot shifts to left foreground, facing right | Badge-making area spotlighted | Introduce the missing-badge mission | Tap the badge area |
| D3 | Mascot remains left but lowers slightly | Background returns to normal | Ask what to call the learner | Safe name/avatar choice |
| D4 | Mascot shrinks to 70% and moves to lower corner | Whole scene clear | Invite silent observation | Free look; tap one detail |
| D5 | Mascot moves opposite each target | Six activity clusters spotlighted sequentially | Establish scene map with short contextual language | Tap highlighted cluster |
| D6 | Mascot near lower center, facing the selected character | Selected child spotlighted; rest dimmed 30% | Model introduction and show attention to speaker | Listen / replay |
| D7 | Mascot turns toward learner, larger close-up | Scene softly dimmed | Ask learner’s station choice and reason | Choice + spoken response |
| D8 | Mascot steps aside to edge, smaller | Six-icon speaking prompt highlighted | Invite baseline introduction without dominating | Record response |
| D9 | Mascot returns close, relaxed breathing idle | Scene warm and lightly dimmed | Celebrate effort and ask reflection | Confidence + goal |

## Content required for Session 1

### Visual

- Existing Welcome Fair big-picture scene
- Mascot cutout in at least three expressions: welcoming, curious/listening, celebrating
- Blank badge and one unlocked hobby badge symbol
- Six simple activity/station icons
- Six-icon baseline speaking prompt card
- Hotspot masks for the six station clusters and at least five individual people

### Audio

- Light fair ambience loop with no intelligible background speech
- Mascot dialogue lines for all guided phases
- Three model child introductions
- Five listen-and-find clue recordings
- Core interest-word recordings
- Correct, retry, replay, and transition micro-lines

### Text/data

- Dialogue phase definitions
- Hotspot geometry and accessible labels
- Five listening items with evidence tags
- Six interest retrieval items
- Personal response prompt and optional sentence frame
- Baseline response definition
- Reflection choices and next-step options

## Differentiation within the track

| Moment | On-demand support | Core | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Picture description | Word bank + sentence starter | Keyword prompt | Add position or reason |
| Listening | Replay + visual narrowing after first attempt | Full scene and two plays | One play before selection |
| Station choice | Choose from sentence endings | Open reason | Add comparison with another station |
| Baseline recording | Three icons + model replay | Six icons, no script | Add a follow-up question to the mascot |

### Important baseline rule

The baseline prompt must remain supportive but minimally taught. It is evidence of the learner’s starting point, not a performance they should perfect in Session 1.

---

## Analytics and mastery signals

Capture only signals that improve later teaching:

- hotspot clusters visited;
- listen-and-find first-attempt accuracy and replay use;
- interest vocabulary retrieval;
- whether the learner produced a sentence, detail, or reason;
- baseline recording reference and duration;
- selected support level;
- confidence response and next-step goal.

Do not convert observation time, exploratory taps, or use of replay into negative performance signals.

---

## Session 1 definition of done

- The student experiences a continuous mission despite activity-engine transitions.
- The mascot guides attention without covering the current visual target.
- The learner speaks at least four times, including one baseline recording.
- Every scored interaction maps to a stated objective.
- At least one open response permits multiple valid answers.
- Background dimming and spotlighting remain readable and accessible.
- Character movement supports story meaning rather than functioning as decoration.
- The session can be completed with audio muted through captions and visual instructions.
- Reduced-motion mode replaces travel animations with short fades or instant placement.
- The session ends with evidence usable to personalize the next lesson.
