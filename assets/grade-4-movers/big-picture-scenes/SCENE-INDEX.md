# Grade 4 Movers Big Picture Scenes

These 16:9, text-free scenes were generated with the built-in image generation workflow for reusable speaking, listening, reading, writing, and assessment activities.

## Shared art direction

- Polished children's editorial illustration with soft dimensional digital-gouache texture
- Contemporary, international Grade 4 setting; engaging but not babyish
- Slightly elevated three-quarter viewpoint with readable foreground, middle ground, and background
- Inclusive character representation and a recurring original blue learning-companion mascot
- Teal, coral, sunny yellow, leafy green, and natural environmental colors
- No embedded text, labels, brands, flags, logos, speech bubbles, or watermarks

## Reusable activity layers

Each scene is intended to support hotspot discovery, listen-and-find, true/false correction, describe-and-guess, information gaps, personal connection questions, picture-change variants, odd-one-out reasoning, story prompts, guided writing, and open speaking missions.
