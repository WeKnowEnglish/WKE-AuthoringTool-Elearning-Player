# Book 2 art prompts — Where Is Milo?

## Shared art direction

- Use case: `illustration-story`
- Asset type: landscape chapter illustration for an A1 children’s easy reader
- Reference image: the supplied Book 2 cover establishes the characters, dog, palette, and polished 3D storybook style
- Characters: Mia is a primary-age girl with long brown hair, a blue headband, purple hoodie, blue jeans, and purple shoes; Leo is a primary-age boy with short dark-brown hair, green hoodie, blue jeans, and blue shoes; Milo is a small tan-and-white dog with floppy brown ears and a blue collar
- Style: cheerful polished children’s picture-book illustration, soft 3D/cartoon rendering, expressive faces, warm natural light, clean readable action
- Composition: 16:9 landscape, one clear story beat, important clue visible, room around the subjects for responsive cropping
- Constraints: no text, letters, signs, captions, speech bubbles, logos, watermark, frightening expressions, extra children, duplicated people, or changed clothing

## Character reference

Full-body turnaround-style cast sheet on a warm off-white studio background. Show Mia, Leo, and Milo standing separately with consistent scale, front three-quarter view, plus three small expression portraits for each child (curious, worried, happy). Milo is alert and friendly. No labels or text.

## Chapter scenes

1. **An Empty Basket** — In a bright family kitchen, Mia and Leo kneel beside Milo’s empty basket near the window. His blue ball is inside, the back door is open, and breakfast bowls are on the table. They look worried but calm.
2. **Searching the House** — Split-depth home scene with Mia looking under the living-room sofa in the foreground and Leo checking behind a bedroom curtain through an open doorway. Two shoes are under the sofa and a teddy bear is on the bed.
3. **Clues in the Garden** — In a wet green garden after rain, Mia and Leo follow small muddy paw prints between flowerpots toward a gap under a white fence. Milo’s yellow toy duck lies beside the gap.
4. **Around the Neighborhood** — Outside a friendly neighborhood bakery, Mia shows Milo’s picture to an adult baker while Leo looks toward a nearby bus stop and leafy park entrance. Keep any papers and signs blank.
5. **A Bark by the Shed** — Beside a pond in a sunny park, Mia and Leo listen at a small closed wooden shed. One little brown dog paw is visible under the door. Ducks are on the pond; the mood is hopeful, not scary.
6. **Two Animals Go Home** — Beside the open park shed, Mia and Leo hug Milo while a happy family reunites with a tiny gray kitten wearing a red tag. Warm relief and celebration, with the park worker nearby.
