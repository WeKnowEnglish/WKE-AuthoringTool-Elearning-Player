# Teacher Review — Daily Bakery Quest

_Status: draft — derived from golden reference, not canonical._

## Objective

Students name bread, milk, eggs, and jam and use simple bakery sentences.

## Loop summary

| Phase | Time | What happens |
|-------|------|----------------|
| Story | ~1 min | Missing recipe cards; meet bread, milk, eggs, jam |
| Presentation | ~5 min | T/F, fill blanks, spelling, MCQ |
| Explorer | ~10 min | Collect ingredients on bakery map; cloze with Mai |
| Reflection | ~1 min | Self-check + one retrieval |

## Materials

- Device with Lesson Player pilot route or imported screens
- Optional: printed word cards for bread, milk, eggs, jam

## Teacher moves

1. Preview story hook — ask "What is missing?"
2. After presentation, oral drill: "I need ___."
3. During explorer, encourage full sentences, not single words
4. Reflection: praise effort, not only correct answers

## Standards alignment

- Four Strands: input (story), study (presentation), output + fluency (explorer)
