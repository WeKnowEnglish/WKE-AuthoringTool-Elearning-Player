# Grade 4 Movers — Unit 1, Session 3

## Find Something in Common

### Learner promise

By the end of this session, the learner can ask whether somebody likes an activity, understand a short yes/no answer, and say what they have in common with a friend.

### Progression from Sessions 1 and 2

- Session 1: express a personal preference with **I like ...**.
- Session 2: ask **What do you like doing?**, listen for details, and report **He/She likes ...**.
- Session 3: follow up with **Do you like ...?**, answer **Yes, I do / No, I don't**, and connect people with **We both like ...**.

### Cambridge Movers alignment

- Speaking: answer and ask simple personal questions; give linked personal information.
- Listening: identify affirmative and negative answers and the activity being discussed.
- Reading: understand a short friendship message and locate shared interests.
- Writing: produce a short two-person exchange and one shared-interest sentence.

### Success criteria

The learner can:

1. build and say **Do you like + activity-ing?**;
2. distinguish **Yes, I do** from **No, I don't** in a short response;
3. respond naturally with **Me too** or **That's okay**;
4. complete **We both like + activity-ing**;
5. record one short question-and-response turn.
