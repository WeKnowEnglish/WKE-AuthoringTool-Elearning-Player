# Grade 4 Session 2 — Character dialogue

Status: **Approved for first polished generation.**

Session: Unit 1, Session 2 — Find a Fair Friend  
Voices: Keelan/Coral, Mia/Nova, Leo/Verse, Sam/Alloy  
Level: Cambridge Movers; short, clear, speaking-first English

## Recording principles

- Every clip leads to one obvious action or response.
- The three children sound like friendly peers, not quiz presenters.
- Retry language makes the listening problem shared and solvable.
- Buttons, scores, timers, transcripts, and privacy text remain on-screen only.
- All character voices are fictional and AI-generated.
