## Match and common ground

### `s3-match-intro`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Who said yes to your favourite activity? Join the two matching badges.”
**Direction:** Clear visual puzzle instruction.

### `s3-common-painting`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Mia and you both like painting animals. Say: We both like painting animals.”
**Direction:** Stress “both” and model the complete sentence.

### `s3-common-football`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Leo and you both like playing football. Say: We both like playing football.”
**Direction:** Stress “both” and model the complete sentence.

### `s3-common-reading`
**Speaker:** Keelan

**Direction:** Stress “both” and model the complete sentence.

### `s3-record-common`

**Direction:** Proud, compact celebration.

## Check-up and reflection

### `s3-check-intro`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Three quick clues before the Friendship Circle opens. Ready?”
**Direction:** Game energy without pressure.

### `s3-check-football`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Who says yes to playing football: Mia, Leo, or Sam?”
**Direction:** Even emphasis across all names.

### `s3-check-negative`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Which answer means no: Yes, I do; No, I don’t; or Me too?”
**Direction:** Keep all choices neutral.

### `s3-check-shared`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Which sentence shows a shared interest: We both like reading; Sam likes reading; or Do you like reading?”
**Direction:** Keep all choices neutral; make “both” clear but not revealing.

### `s3-check-retry`
**Speaker:** Keelan
**Line:** “Almost! Use the picture clue and listen once more.”
**Direction:** Collaborative and non-punitive.

### `s3-check-correct`

**Direction:** Quick reward.

### `s3-check-complete`
