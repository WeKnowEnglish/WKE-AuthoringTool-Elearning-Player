5. Finish your friend badge

Write about you. Use complete sentences.

Write neatly. Check capitals, spelling, and full stops.

Meet Me! — Answer key

Teacher copy

1. Match the question to the answer

  1. What's your name? → C My name's Linh.
  2. How old are you? → E I'm nine years old.
  3. What do you like doing? → A I like drawing and painting.
  4. Where do you live? → D I live in Hanoi.
  5. Can you swim? → B Yes, I can.
  6. Have you got a pet? → F Yes, I have. I've got a cat.

2. Circle the correct word

  1. I am nine years old.
  2. She likes reading.
  3. I have got a football.
  4. He can play chess.
  5. My favourite sport is basketball.

3. Complete the sentences

  1. I like painting and I like drawing. (and)
  2. I like football but I don't like basketball. (but)
  3. I like cooking because it's fun. (because)
  4. I can swim quite well. (can)
  5. What do you like doing? (like)

4. Put the words in order

  1. What's your name?
  2. How old are you?
  3. What do you like doing?
  4. Can you dance?
  5. What about you?

5. Finish your friend badge

Student's own answers. Check for a complete sentence, capital letter, and full stop.
