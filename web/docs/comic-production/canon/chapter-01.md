## Page beats

### Cover

Introduces the four friends, valley setting, spacecraft mystery, and the values of curiosity, courage, friendship, and adventure.

### Page 1

The friends spend time in the meadow after school. Leo and Ethan pretend to battle as space commanders while Mia and Zara read science and space books.

**Visual lock:** In panel 3, Mia is seated on the left in purple. Zara is seated on the right in her yellow sun shirt and blue jean shorts.

### Page 2

Mia argues that aliens could not exist or would be too distant to meet. Ethan refuses to give up hope.

### Page 3

A spacecraft crosses the sky and crashes near the woods. The children decide to investigate together.

### Page 4

They reach the crashed saucer with their backpacks, enter, and discover a glowing blue crystal. The backpacks remain with the children; there are no duplicate loose packs at the entrance.

### Page 5

Movement in a nearby tree hollow draws them away from the ship. Golden eyes and small fangs appear in the darkness.

### Page 6

The children run. Keelan emerges from the hollow and calls, “Wait!” The chapter ends on the encounter.

## Learning opportunities

- Predict whether aliens exist and explain an opinion.
- Retell a sequence using first, next, then, and finally.
- Understand imperatives such as run, wait, and stay together.
- Discuss safe choices when investigating something unknown.
- Compare Mia’s skepticism with Ethan’s hope.
