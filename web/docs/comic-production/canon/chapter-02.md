## Canonical six-page sequence

1. **Crash site:** Keelan asks the children not to run, introduces himself, explains that he learned their language by listening, and asks for help.
2. **Home and help:** Keelan makes a small projection of his home planet, becomes weak, and the children assign concrete help: Mia brings food, Zara brings water, Leo brings the purple star blanket, and Ethan helps carry Keelan.
3. **Trusted adult and shelter:** The group crosses the fields under a brown plaid carrying blanket. They tell Grandpa Minh that their injured friend needs help. He directs them to Leo's treehouse and says he will tell their families.
4. **Food and questions:** The children share food, ask about Keelan's distant home, and prepare to leave. Grandpa Minh supervises; Mia may remain for ten more minutes.
5. **Quiet search:** Mia notices that Keelan is worried. He uses one small localized blue search spell and admits that he cannot find anyone like him.
6. **Disappearance:** Mia leaves Keelan safely with Grandpa Minh nearby. The children return before school, find Keelan gone, and follow faint four-toed traces toward the hills.

## Locked continuity

- Crash-site order begins `Ethan - Leo - Mia - Zara - Keelan`.
- The ship remains screen-left/west; the hollow and Keelan remain screen-right/east.
- Mia wears purple, Zara yellow with jean shorts, Leo blue, and Ethan green.
- All four children keep their approved color-coded backpacks. No loose duplicate packs appear by the ship.
- The Page 3 wooden cabin treehouse is canonical. It has a platform, ladder, warm plank interior, round window, shelves, lanterns, and one sleeping corner.
- Grandpa Minh is the trusted adult. He knows where the children and Keelan are and tells their families.
- The brown plaid blanket is used for carrying and concealment. Leo's purple star blanket is the bed blanket and remains through the morning.
- Mia owns the food task, Zara the water task, Leo the blanket task, and Ethan the carrying/bed-preparation task.
- Keelan's magic is small, blue-white, localized, tiring, and never room- or valley-scale while he is weak.
- Keelan has blue fuzzy fur, orange star markings, four fingers, and four toes. Morning evidence is fur and four-toed residue, never feathers or three-toed prints.
- The damaged ship and blue crystal remain at the crash site. The children know the location; no later chapter may move them without a visible story beat.

## Language and learning focus

- **Communicative goal:** Ask about someone's home and offer practical help.
- **Core frames:** `Are you ...?`, `I can ...`, `I can't ...`, `I'll bring ...`, `We have to ...`.
- **Target words:** help, language, listening, planet, weak, blanket, safe, worried, trail, hills.
- Each page has one vocabulary set and one discussion prompt in its overlay.
- Dialogue is short, explicit, and assigned to one speaker with a unique reading order.

## Technical release rules

- Art and lettering remain separate; the clean PNGs are never replaced with flattened dialogue masters.
