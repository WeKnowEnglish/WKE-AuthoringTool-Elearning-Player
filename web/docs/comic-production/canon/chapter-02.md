## Page 1 - confirmed sequence

1. Keelan calls, “Wait! Please don’t run!”
2. The children react: “It can talk…” and “That’s impossible.”
3. Keelan reassures them: “I’m not going to hurt you.”
4. Keelan says, “My name is Keelan.” One child observes, “He’s kind of cute…”
5. Keelan reveals, “I can use a little magic… but not much.” A child responds, “Magic?!”
6. The children notice he is hurt. Keelan explains, “My ship crashed. I’m weak.” They consider helping him.

## Canon established on this page

- The alien’s name is Keelan.
- Keelan uses he/him pronouns in the children’s dialogue.
- Keelan is peaceful.
- Keelan can speak the children’s language.
- Keelan has limited magic.
- Keelan is weak after the crash.
- The children begin moving from fear toward empathy and cooperation.

## Language opportunities
