# Chapter 2 Remediation Specification

**Status:** Approved working specification for the v1 correction pass

**Date:** 2026-08-03

**Learners:** Ages 9-12, CEFR A1-A2

## Locked production decisions

- The six submitted story pages are the story direction; the duplicate Page 6 upload is not canonical.
- The submitted five-panel Page 1 supersedes the older working Page 1 after the corrected art and overlay are approved.
- Clean art contains no speech bubbles, captions, panel numbers, sound effects, or other lettering.
- All lettering and learning supports live in overlay JSON.
- The crash-site map remains ship west/screen-left and hollow/Keelan east/screen-right until an established axis change.
- Chapter 2 opens with the Chapter 1 closing order: Ethan, Leo, Mia, Zara, Keelan.
- Every child keeps their approved color-coded backpack until the treehouse, where the bags hang on fixed wall pegs.
- Page 3's built wooden cabin treehouse is the environment canon. It is not a natural hollow in the trunk.
- The hiding blanket is brown/green plaid. The bed blanket is purple with small gold stars and is brought by Leo.
- Mia brings food, Zara brings water, Leo brings the bed blanket, and Ethan helps carry Keelan and prepare the sleeping area.
- Grandpa Minh is a trusted village adult. He knows the children are helping an injured friend, tells their families, and waits nearby while Mia finishes speaking with Keelan.
- Keelan's magic remains localized: a palm-sized home projection, a small search field around his cushion, and faint nearby four-toed residue.
- Keelan has fuzzy fur, not feathers. Any evidence left behind is a small fur tuft.
- The final path is inferred from nearby residue pointing toward the hills; it is not a glowing road across the valley.

## Fixed treehouse anchors

- Rectangular wooden cabin supported by a large living tree.
- Narrow exterior platform with a simple rail.
- One straight wooden ladder on the left side of the entrance.
- Warm string lights under the platform and around the doorway.
- Rectangular doorway with rounded top corners.
- Interior floorboards run from doorway toward the rear wall.
- One round window on the rear wall.
- Low sleeping corner under the round window: green cushion, blue star pillow, purple star blanket.
- Shelves on the right wall; drawing/poster area on the left wall.
- Four color-coded backpack pegs beside the doorway.
- Lantern normally sits beside the doorway unless a character carries it.

## Corrected page beats

### Page 1 - Please do not run

Five horizontal panels. Match the source emotional progression. Panel 1 continues directly from Chapter 1 with Ethan, Leo, Mia, and Zara facing Keelan; ship left, hollow right. Keelan reassures them and asks for help. No magic appears. Backpacks remain with their owners.

### Page 2 - A small view of home

Six panels. Keelan confirms that he is from another planet and makes a small palm-sized projection of a visibly non-Earth world. The projection fades when he coughs. The children choose concrete help: Mia/food, Zara/water, Leo/purple star blanket, Ethan/carrying and setup. They support Keelan toward the village.

### Page 3 - A safe place

Six panels. At dusk, the children carry Keelan under the brown plaid blanket. His magic does not show through it. Keelan asks about hiding; the children explain that they will tell a trusted adult. They meet Grandpa Minh and honestly say that a friend is hurt. He directs them to Leo's treehouse and says he will tell their families. They arrive with the correctly assigned supplies and settle Keelan in the fixed interior.

### Page 4 - Food and questions

Six panels. Everyone eats in the fixed interior. The children ask about Keelan's home. A visible Grandpa Minh calls from the doorway that it is time to go home. He allows Mia ten more minutes and waits below. Leo leaves the purple star blanket with Keelan. The four backpacks remain identifiable.

### Page 5 - Anyone like me

Five panels. Mia asks whether Keelan is awake and notices that he is worried. Keelan makes a small localized search projection. A brief cyan field surrounds only Keelan and the cushion. The attempt fails. He admits that he cannot find anyone like him.

### Page 6 - The trail to the hills

Six panels. Mia leaves by the established ladder while Grandpa Minh waits below. The four friends return before school wearing their approved backpacks. Keelan is gone. The purple blanket remains; only blue fur tufts and faint four-toed cyan prints remain. A few traces lead from the platform toward the hills. The children decide to find him.
