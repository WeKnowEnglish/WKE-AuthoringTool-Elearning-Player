# Comic Page Continuity and Learning Checklist

Complete this checklist before a page becomes canonical.

## Story connection

- [ ] The first panel matches the previous page’s final positions, emotions, props, and lighting.
- [ ] The page has one clear story job.
- [ ] Every action has a visible cause or an understandable transition.
- [ ] The final panel creates a clear reason to turn the page.

## Character continuity

- [ ] Mia is purple and matches her approved hair, clip, clothing, footwear, and props.
- [ ] Zara is yellow and matches her approved hair, headband, clothing, footwear, and props.
- [ ] Leo is blue and matches his approved hair, wave shirt, shorts, footwear, and props.
- [ ] Ethan is green and matches his approved hair, mountain shirt, shorts, footwear, and props.
- [ ] Keelan matches his approved height, ears, markings, eyes, fingers, fangs, tail, and magic effect.
- [ ] All human characters clearly look 9-12 years old.
- [ ] Character order and screen direction do not jump without explanation.

## Environment and props

- [ ] The spacecraft silhouette, entrance, damage, and blue lights match the approved model.
- [ ] The ship, tree hollow, paths, and landmarks match the scene map.
- [ ] Backpacks, books, crystal, and other props remain where the previous page left them.
- [ ] Time of day, weather, shadows, and light direction remain coherent.

## Language and learning

- [ ] The page is understandable to A1 learners through image context.
- [ ] Most dialogue is independently readable by A2 learners.
- [ ] Each balloon contains one clear idea.
- [ ] New vocabulary is visually supported or naturally repeated.
- [ ] Pronouns have obvious referents.
- [ ] Each balloon tail clearly identifies the speaker.
- [ ] The text models useful, natural communication.
- [ ] Spelling, punctuation, names, and contractions match the approved script.

## Layout and technical quality

- [ ] Canvas is 1122 x 1402 px unless an approved exception is recorded.
- [ ] Panel borders, gutters, and parchment numbers are consistent.
- [ ] Reading order is unmistakable.
- [ ] Lettering is readable on a tablet and classroom display.
- [ ] Text stays inside the safe area.
- [ ] No cropped hands, unintended extra fingers, duplicate characters, or floating objects.
- [ ] No visual detail contradicts the script or canon record.

## Approval record

- [ ] Writer approved.
- [ ] Language/curriculum reviewer approved.
- [ ] Visual continuity reviewer approved.
- [ ] Final canonical filename and page number verified.
- [ ] Alternate versions moved to `alternates`.
- [ ] Chapter canon record updated.
