## Human cast locks

| Character | Hair and face | Clothing | Required props when carried |

| Mia | Long straight black hair; exactly one purple clip on her right side | Purple floral/space dress; purple socks and high-tops | Purple Saturn backpack; science book |
| Zara | Very long dark curly hair; exactly one yellow headband | Yellow sun shirt; blue shorts; yellow shoes | Yellow/orange sun backpack; space book |
| Leo | Short tousled black hair | Blue wave shirt; dark shorts; striped socks; blue high-tops | Blue wave backpack; wooden pretend-play stick when scripted |
| Ethan | Curly brown hair; light freckles | Green mountain shirt; khaki cargo shorts; striped socks; green high-tops | Green mountain backpack; wooden pretend-play stick when scripted |

The four children are approximately the same age and height. Natural differences of no more than about five percent are allowed, but no child should suddenly read as much older, younger, or adult-sized.

## Keelan locks

- Small blue fuzzy alien, approximately 55-60% of a child’s standing height from feet to ear tips.
- Large pointed ears with a relaxed outward angle.
- Large amber-gold eyes with dark pupils.
- Orange star markings, including the prominent centered forehead star and rear-crown star shown on the approved turnaround.
- Four digits on each hand and four toes on each foot.
- Two small upper fangs.
- Medium fuzzy tail with a gently upturned tip.
- Blue-white magic: a small floating spiral/orb with soft cyan sparks, never fire-like.
- Marking rule: preserve the large anchor stars and the overall distribution from the sheet; do not add new large markings or move the forehead star.

## Expression library

Use the expression rows as the facial-language standard:

- Mia: neutral, curious/delighted, skeptical, surprised, worried.
- Zara: neutral, delighted, thoughtful, concerned, surprised.
- Leo: neutral, excited, determined, surprised, worried.
- Ethan: neutral, hopeful, determined, excited, afraid.
- Keelan: neutral/friendly, worried, weak, surprised, with separate hand and magic callouts.

## Age-appropriateness rule

All human characters must clearly appear 9-12 years old, with child proportions, practical fully covering clothing, and age-appropriate expressions and poses. Do not use adult body proportions, glamour posing, romantic framing, or sexualized styling.

## Approval method

For every final page, compare each character against the approved front, side, three-quarter, back, expression, and prop references. Reject pages with changed identity colors, symbols, hair, age, body proportions, or anatomy. Any intentional redesign creates a new numbered model-sheet version and requires an update to the canon record.
