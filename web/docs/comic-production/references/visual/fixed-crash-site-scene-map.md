# Fixed Crash-Site Scene Map

**Status:** Production canon v1.1  
**Applies to:** Chapter 1, Page 4 through the opening crash-site sequence

## Fixed geography

- The crashed saucer is west/left.
- The tree-root hollow is east/right.
- The ship entrance faces east toward the hollow.
- Mia, Zara, Leo, and Ethan each keep their own color-coded backpack. No duplicate backpacks are placed outside the ship entrance.
- The glowing blue crystal remains inside the ship unless a page explicitly moves it.
- Keelan begins at the hollow threshold, facing west toward the children.
- The children stand between the ship and Keelan, facing east.

## Opening lineup

The final Chapter 1 lineup is the visual continuity authority:

- Ethan: green.
- Leo: blue.
- Mia: purple.
- Zara: yellow.
- Keelan: east/right near the hollow.

Small natural regrouping is allowed only after a panel shows movement.

## Camera rule

- The main story axis runs west-east between the ship and hollow.
- Default cameras remain south of that axis so the ship reads on screen-left and Keelan/hollow read on screen-right.
- Do not cross the axis without an establishing panel or visible movement that reorients the reader.

## Prop rule

The backpacks stay with their owners unless a panel explicitly shows a child removing one. A front-facing or tightly cropped pose may obscure a worn backpack, but no loose duplicate pack may appear at the ship entrance.
